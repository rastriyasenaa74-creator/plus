ABOUT PLUS MEDISTOCK

Technology for more organised pharmacy operations.

Our focus is simple: make everyday medical store administration clearer, more structured and easier to manage.

Who We Are

PLUS MEDISTOCK is a pharmacy management software project designed to help medical stores organise routine business operations through a clear digital workspace.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to bring medicine records, stock visibility, suppliers, customers and store workflows into a more organised system.

Our Mission

To develop practical and accessible tools that help pharmacy teams maintain organised business records and review operational information.

Our Vision

To build a dependable pharmacy operations platform that can grow with independent stores and multi-store businesses while keeping usability, privacy and accountability central.

What We Value

  • Clarity: understandable information and straightforward workflows.
  • Privacy: store data should be isolated and access-controlled.
  • Reliability: features should be tested before being described as complete.
  • Improvement: keep learning from real pharmacy workflows.
Important: PLUS MEDISTOCK is business administration software. It does not provide medical advice, verify prescriptions or replace the professional responsibilities of a licensed pharmacist.
